University of Waterloo team project

EcoNode

Developing offline computer-vision guidance for apple-tree pruning with off-the-shelf AR glasses.

Try the guidance walkthrough

The wearable concept

A local model would locate candidate branch features. An expert-reviewed decision layer would determine when the glasses can present a cue. The orchard worker makes the final pruning decision.

Offline inference, wearable integration, and field evaluation are planned milestones.
