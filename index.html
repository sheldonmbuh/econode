EcoNode

AI pruning guidance for orchard workers, delivered through AR glasses.

The idea

Pruning well takes years of experience, and orchards struggle to find enough skilled pruners. EcoNode puts a computer vision model, trained on expert pruning decisions, into AR glasses worn by the worker. The glasses show which branches to cut. The system runs offline, at the edge, because orchards do not have reliable connectivity.

Recognition

Winner of the University of Waterloo Velocity Agri-Innovation Challenge, March 2026. Validated with Steve Duff and Cesar Cappa of OMAFRA.

Where it stands

Data collection this winter pruning season, first working demo in January, field tests with growers in February and March. Built by Sheldon Mbuh, Gursimran, and Harkamal.
